## Confirmed causes and changes

1. Parallel cavity deep-end accesses used independently chosen junction depths.
   The connector from one could climb through another cavity's protected zone.
   `cavity_access._common_axial_candidates()` intersects the actual full-cylinder
   intervals, including the bound closure engagement, and supplies shared-plane
   alternatives. Interior and boundary alternatives use existing explicit `j`
   variant syntax; no schema/setting is added. Eligibility and stop depths of
   the cavity accesses are unchanged.

2. MB's simple coaxial drilling continued through its last window to the nominal
   centre/full-bore endpoint. `_source_safe_coaxial_stops()` adds real shortened
   drilling candidates from the same functional entry. It searches the actual
   fixed protected source cuts, keeps all other obstruction checks, and runs the
   full ordinary screen on the result. Bounding geometry is only a possible
   first-contact lower bound; exact volume/opening and all-terminal connectivity
   remain mandatory. It preserves the Ø8 bore and needs no new construction
   entry. The accepted MB depth is 57.848341 mm instead of 72.973630 mm.

3. Generic orthogonal holes rounded source/junction coordinates to six decimal
   places while axial accesses retained full source precision. The observed Y
   difference was about 0.000000227 mm. OCCT could return an empty intersection
   between otherwise crossing cylinders. `propose()` now retains the supplied
   coordinates, rather than moving an axis through rounding. Existing authored
   and committed cuts are never regenerated by this change.

4. A production solid that was initially valid became invalid after contact and
   protection validation. The Boolean result shared topology with operands used
   by those later intersections. `build_geometry()` now detaches the exact
   production topology/geometry using `copy(mesh=False)`. This is no healing,
   tolerance relaxation or altered cut. Final validation preserves validity.

5. The analytic AI layout sample omitted simple coaxial candidates and could
   exhaust its retained sample on unsafe raw variants before considering legal
   stopping/common-plane strategies. It now includes the shared strategies,
   retaining source-clear samples first, with rejected samples as diagnostics
   or unresolved fallback. The 32 sample-attempt, eight retained-sample,
   eight-layout and normal Router/Exact budgets remain bounded and unchanged.
